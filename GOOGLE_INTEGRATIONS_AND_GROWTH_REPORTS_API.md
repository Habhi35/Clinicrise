# ClinicRise Google Integrations & Monthly Growth Reports API

## 1. Overview & Architecture

ClinicRise provides an automated, multi-tenant growth engine tailored specifically for outpatient healthcare practices, specialty polyclinics, and dental clinics. This module delivers two interconnected engines:

1. **Advanced Google Integrations (`gbp` app)**:
   - **Google Business Profile (GBP) API Management**: Direct OAuth and location integration, automated 30-day insight synchronization (Maps impressions, Search impressions, phone calls, driving directions, website clicks).
   - **Google Patient Reviews & HIPAA-Compliant AI Replies**: Synchronizes 1-to-5 star Google reviews and generates empathetic, professional response drafts that strictly safeguard patient privacy by avoiding disclosure of medical conditions, diagnoses, or clinical history (compliant with HIPAA and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act).
   - **Google Posts & Clinical Updates**: Doctors and practice teams can publish clinic offers, OPD announcements, and health advice with Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons (`BOOK`, `CALL`, `LEARN_MORE`).
   - **Google Search Console (GSC) Analytics**: Ingests organic search impressions, clicks, click-through rates (CTR), and average search position for high-intent medical queries (e.g., *"dentist near me"*, *"root canal treatment cost"*).
   - **Local 3-Pack Rank Tracking**: Automated rank checker tracking target specialty keywords across localized map grids, calculating pack dominance %, Top 3 presence, and competitor positions.

2. **Monthly Growth Reports & Publication-Grade PDF Engine (`reports` app)**:
   - **Clinical Performance Synthesis**: Aggregates patient bookings, attendance rate, new patient acquisition, Google profile views, organic search queries, and local map dominance into a structured monthly growth summary.
   - **Doctor-Centric Recommendations**: Synthesizes prioritized, high-leverage clinical and marketing action items for doctors to execute in the upcoming month.
   - **Publication-Ready Vector PDF Generation**: Engineered with Python `reportlab` (v4.5.1). Delivers multi-page, branded PDFs featuring clinical palettes (Slate Navy, Medical Teal, Slate White), 4-column KPI metric cards, clean alternating-row tables, executive summaries, and confidentiality legal footers.

## 2. Multi-Tenant Architecture & Security

Every Google integration and growth report is strictly isolated by clinic practice:
- **Tenant Isolation**: Every database model inherits from `TenantAwareModel`, linking records to `practices.Practice`.
- **Tenant Context Resolution**: Endpoints require `IsPracticeMember` and resolve practice context from the `X-Practice-ID` header, user session, or single-membership association. Attempts to access or modify another practice's GBP connection, reviews, or PDF reports are denied with `403 Forbidden` or `404 Not Found`.
- **Healthcare Privacy Rules**: Review replies generated by the AI engine never echo or acknowledge sensitive diagnostic information, protecting clinics from HIPAA and DPDP non-compliance.

## 5. ReportLab PDF Generation Architecture

The PDF generation engine is implemented in [reports/services/report_pdf_service.py](file:///c:/Users/yella/ClinicRise/reports/services/report_pdf_service.py):

- **Page Layout**: A4 page geometry with 0.5-inch margins for compact, multi-section printable reporting.
- **Color Hierarchy**:
  - `COLOR_PRIMARY`: `#0F172A` (Deep Slate Navy)
  - `COLOR_ACCENT`: `#0D9488` (Medical Teal)
  - `COLOR_SECONDARY`: `#2563EB` (Professional Blue)
  - `COLOR_BG_CARD`: `#F8FAFC` (Light Slate Background)
  - `COLOR_BORDER`: `#CBD5E1` (Table Grid & Card Borders)
- **Document Sections**:
  1. **Branded Practice Header**: Clinic name, location, generation timestamp, and ClinicRise Healthcare Growth Engine banner.
  2. **Executive KPI Cards**: 4-column balanced grid highlighting Consultations, Google Profile Views, Direct Phone Calls, and Average Local 3-Pack Rank.
  3. **Executive Performance Summary**: Synthesized natural-language overview for doctor review.
  4. **Table 1: Patient Acquisition & Consultations**: Completed OPD, new patient charts, online scheduling, walk-ins, and attendance rate.
  5. **Table 2: Google Business Profile Discovery**: Maps impressions, search impressions, inbound calls, driving directions, and Google review volume.
  6. **Table 3: Google Search Console Top Medical Queries**: Exact search queries, organic clicks, impressions, CTR, and average search rank.
  7. **Prioritized Growth Recommendations**: Numbered clinical and digital marketing actions for clinic staff.
  8. **Confidentiality & Compliance Footer**: Legal disclaimer for practice internal use.
